Frequently Asked Questions for College Students

Q1 What time does the college start and end?
▼
Regular college classes are generally conducted from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Q2 Where can students contact the college office?
▼
Students can visit the administrative office during official working hours for assistance.
Q3 How can I pay my semester fees?
▼
Semester fees can be paid using the online student portal or through the college accounts office.
Q4 What should I do if I lose my college ID card?
▼
Report the lost ID card to the college administration and request a replacement card.
Q5 What facilities are available in the library?
▼
The library provides textbooks, reference books, newspapers, journals and study resources.
Q6 How can I submit a leave application?
▼
Students can submit their leave application through the student portal or to the concerned faculty member.
Q7 Where can I find the examination timetable?
▼
The examination timetable is normally published on the college website and student notice board.
Q8 Where can students access learning resources?
▼
Notes, assignments and other learning resources can be accessed through the library and student portal.
